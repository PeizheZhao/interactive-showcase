How to read this map

The colored bands are qualitative research-activity and practical-relevance periods, not publication counts. A technology can remain useful long after its “peak” period. Many modern systems combine multiple families—for example, a Transformer backbone with latent diffusion or flow matching.

Historical transition

GAN/VAE-era systems gave way to diffusion as the dominant general-purpose image-generation paradigm, while autoregressive Transformers dominated text.

Current convergence

Transformer backbones now sit inside diffusion and flow systems, while image-token autoregressive models are converging with unified multimodal models.

Most promising direction

Flow Matching / Rectified Flow, efficient few-step sampling, native multimodality, and video/world-model generation are among the strongest forward-looking themes.
